References

This page lists sources of information and images used throughout the EduPath Nigeria website.

Data and statistics

  • National Bureau of Statistics (Nigeria) — labour force and unemployment statistics, used as a general reference for graduate underemployment figures cited on the home page.
  • National Universities Commission (NUC) — accreditation information referenced for course and pathway descriptions.
  • Industrial Training Fund (ITF) — vocational training programme information referenced in the Resources directory.
  • Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) and Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) — licensing pathway information referenced in the Path Finder data.
  • Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) — professional qualification pathway referenced in the Path Finder data.

Career pathway and resource information

Career pathway steps, timelines, and salary ranges presented in the Path Finder tool were compiled from publicly available information about Nigerian education and training programmes, combined with the author's professional experience in the Nigerian education sector. These figures are estimates intended for general guidance and are not guarantees of outcomes.
